# BERV 

During the observation of any given star Earth is also moving throughout space, introducing another source of Doppler shifts on the spectra. To remove it, the wavelengths are converted from the reference frame  of the instrument to an inertial one, the barycenter of the solar system. This correction term can be calculated with a precision of 1  $cm/s$ (Wright et al. 2014). However, if there are airmass changes during the observations, the barycentric correction has a wavelength dependence, introducing errors of several $cm/s$ in the correction, as shown by Blackman et al. 2017
