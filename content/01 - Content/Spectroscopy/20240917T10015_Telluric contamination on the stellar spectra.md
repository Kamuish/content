# Telluric contamination on the stellar spectra

If we now focus on the optical wavelengths,  we see that this contamination is mainly caused by water and oxygen, and the absorption can reach saturation in some regions, as seen in Fig. [[#^70f6af]]. However, it is even clearer that the atmosphere poses a more significant challenge in the NIR regions, which is covered by the [[2024_NIRPS|NIRPS]] and [[20240917T10852_SPIROU|SPIROU]].
