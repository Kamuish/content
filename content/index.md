# Who am I

In 2019 I finished an integrated masters in engineering physics in the Faculty of sciences from the University of Porto. My master thesis was done in the Instituto de Astrofísica e Ciências do Espaço (IA), Universidade do Porto, with its goal being the development of a post-processing pipeline to extract light-curves from background stars in the Field-of-View of ESA's CHEOPS mission. After it, I enrolled in the Doctoral Program in Astrophysics in IA/ Faculty of sciences of the University of Porto. Its main goal was the development of new algorithms for the extraction of precise RVs. During the past 4 years I have been working within one of the working groups from ESPRESSO, alongside being a science team member of HARPS-N and a collaborator in the KOBE project. 

Currently, I am a post-doctoral researcher funded by the *FIERCE* ERC project,  contributing to the development of software for PoET, a portuguese-built solar telescope that will be connected to ESPRESSO.
